Analysis of BGP Maximum-Prefix Limits and their impact on network stability. This research examines how outdated prefix limits can lead to unexpected BGP session drops and network instability.

**Key Findings:**
- Analyzed 3.4 TB of full-year RIS data
- Identified cases where limits disconnect networks from major providers
- Increased risk of poor routing decisions and SLA violations
