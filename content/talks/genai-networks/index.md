*Workshop exploring the intersection of Generative AI and network operations*

## Workshop Overview

This workshop brings together researchers, practitioners, and industry experts to discuss the emerging role of **Generative AI (GenAI) and Large Language Models (LLMs)** in networking. The focus is on practical applications, challenges, and opportunities for deploying AI-driven solutions in network management and optimization.

## Key Topics

- **AI-Driven Network Optimization**: Using GenAI to optimize resource allocation and network performance
- **Autonomous Network Management**: How LLMs can assist in network configuration and troubleshooting
- **Network Reasoning and Analysis**: LLMs for understanding complex network behaviors
- **Challenges & Limitations**: Practical constraints in deploying GenAI for networking
- **Future Directions**: Emerging research opportunities at the intersection of AI and networking

## Presentation: Agentic AI in Networking

My contribution focuses on **agentic approaches** to network automation:
- How autonomous AI agents can make network decisions
- Integration with network management systems
- Real-world deployment challenges and solutions
