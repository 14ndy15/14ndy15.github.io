## Motivation

Understanding mobile network behavior at fine granularity—across multiple regions and service types—is essential for network research, optimization, and planning. Yet **comprehensive, high-resolution datasets capturing service-level traffic are scarce**. 

Most available datasets focus on:
- Aggregate traffic (not service-level breakdown)
- Single regions only
- Coarse temporal resolution (hourly or daily)
- Limited service categories

This gap limits research on network traffic characterization, demand prediction, and service-aware optimization.

## Dataset Overview

**NetMob23** provides unprecedented visibility into mobile network traffic with:

- **Multi-region coverage**: Multiple European regions with diverse urban/suburban/rural characteristics
- **Service-level granularity**: Traffic broken down by service category (social media, streaming, messaging, news, etc.)
- **High temporal resolution**: 15-minute traffic samples
- **Production-grade data**: From operational Mobile Network Operator(s)
- **Spatial resolution**: Cell-level traffic characterization

### Key Characteristics

**Scope:**
- Multiple geographical regions (urban, suburban, rural areas)
- Diverse user populations and traffic patterns
- Hundreds of base stations and sectors per region

**Traffic Categories:**
- Social media (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
- Video streaming (Netflix, YouTube, etc.)
- Messaging (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.)
- News and information platforms
- Gaming services
- Cloud services
- Web browsing
- And more...

**Temporal Coverage:**
- Extended observation period capturing seasonal and weekly patterns
- 15-minute granularity enabling study of traffic dynamics
- Complete time series with minimal gaps

## Research Applications

The NetMob23 dataset enables research on:

1. **Traffic Characterization**: Understanding how different services contribute to regional traffic
2. **Demand Prediction**: Building models to forecast service-level demand
3. **Network Optimization**: Service-aware resource allocation and routing
4. **Quality of Experience**: Studying service performance patterns
5. **Energy Efficiency**: Optimizing network operation based on service demands
6. **User Behavior Analysis**: Understanding regional and temporal service consumption patterns
